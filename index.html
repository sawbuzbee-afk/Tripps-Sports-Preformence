Who We Are

Performance Training Built By The Pros

Tripp Sports Performance (TSP) was built on one idea: athletes deserve coaches who've actually done it. Every coach on our staff has played collegiate or professional baseball at a high level — and now they're putting that experience to work for athletes of every age, from first-time players to Team USA competitors.

Whether the goal is a faster fastball, a cleaner swing, a college roster spot, or just building real strength for the first time, TSP builds individualized programs around the athlete, not a one-size-fits-all class.

  • Pitching mechanics & throwing programs
  • Hitting instruction & swing development
  • Fielding & catching fundamentals
  • Strength, conditioning & mobility training
  • Nutrition & recovery coaching
